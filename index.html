What we do

We solve problems that are too hard for generic tools.

We are pragmatic before we are ambitious. Where a proven model or existing API solves your problem well, we use it. The fastest, most reliable path to value is usually the right one. Our specialty begins where those tools reach their limits: problems that demand bespoke systems, original research, and machine learning engineered specifically for your domain.

You bring an intimate understanding of your business; we bring rigorous machine learning and the engineering discipline to put it into production. The result is AI that is precise, explainable, and built for the realities of how you operate.

01

Industrial Visual AI

Machine vision for the factory floor: automated inspection, defect detection, and quality grading on live production lines. Engineered to run on-premise and at the edge, keeping your data and operations entirely under your control.

Example: defect detection on a live apparel line
02

Deep Domain AI

Models built from the ground up on your own data, however complex or imperfect. From predictive analytics and process optimization to anomaly detection, every system is designed alongside your domain experts to capture knowledge no generic model can.

Example: yield forecasting from plantation records
03

Language AI for Sinhala & South Asian Languages

Natural language technology built for the region's languages and context: OCR, speech-to-text, translation, document understanding, and conversational systems that operate fluently in Sinhala and beyond.

Example: Sinhala document OCR with searchable output
04

IoT & Sensor-Integrated AI

Intelligence built from live sensor data: real-time monitoring, sensor fusion, and predictive maintenance, deployed reliably on edge and resource-constrained hardware.

Example: predictive maintenance from vibration sensors
